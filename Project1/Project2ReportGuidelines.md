# 📄 Project 2 Report Guidelines

This report must be written in the **double-column IEEE format**. You must submit your work as a **PDF file** (no source files).

---

## 📝 General Format Requirements

| Requirement | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Length** | Minimum **2 pages** and a maximum of **4 pages**. (References are *not* included in this count.) |
| **Format** | **Double-column IEEE style**. A LaTeX or Word template should be used. |
| **Font** | Standard academic font (e.g., Times New Roman, 10-point, as per the template). |
| **Figures/Tables** | Must be included to present results. |
| **References** | Must use the IEEE citation style. |

---

## 🏗️ Required Report Sections & Content

Your report must be structured logically, following academic paper conventions (e.g., I, II, III, IV, ...).

### 1. Introduction & Motivation

This section must establish the context for your project and its novelty.

* **Discuss Motivation of Mission:** Define the problem your project addresses and the motivation behind solving it. Explain the system or application (e.g., free-floating space manipulator).
* **Discuss Related Work:** Briefly survey what has already been done in this area. This section is critical to demonstrating your understanding of the state-of-the-art.
* **Technical Gap:** Clearly state the limitations of existing work and the specific gap that your project aims to fill.

### 2. Technical Approach & Innovation

This section is the core of your technical contribution.

* **What are you proposing?** Detail your specific technical approach (e.g., the control law, the modeling technique, the hardware design).
* **How is it different from related work?** Clearly articulate the **innovation** or novelty of your method.
* **Innovation/Modeling/Experimentation:** You must demonstrate a significant contribution in at least one of these areas. Explain the specific models derived, the unique algorithms/innovations developed, or the complex experimental/simulation setup used.

### 3. Results and Validation

This section presents the evidence of your work.

* **What is your experiment test?** Describe your simulation or experiment setup, the testing conditions, and the scenarios you evaluated.
* **What were the results?** Present your quantitative and qualitative findings using figures, plots, and tables.
* **How does this validate your simulation?** Analyze the results and explicitly state how they prove the feasibility and success of your proposed approach in meeting the project goals.

---

## 🖼️ Figures Requirement

You must include a **minimum of 3 figures** (and a maximum of 3, not including references) in your report.

* **Fig. 1:** Typically a diagram showing the system architecture, control flow, or overall design.
* **Fig. 2:** A visual of the setup (e.g., a CAD drawing or a simulation screenshot of your spacerobotics system).
* **Fig. 3:** A key plot or graph showing quantitative results and performance (e.g., error tracking, trajectory comparison, or stability metrics).
